How this model is calculated

The vitreous cavity is an Atchison (2006) retinal ellipsoid with Model 2 tilt: 11.5° temporal about the vertical, 3.6° down about the horizontal, and the ellipsoid center 0.5 mm nasal and 0.2 mm inferior of the fovea. The lens stays on the visual axis. The cavity is clipped in front by a Navarro posterior lens if phakic (R = 6 mm, Q = −1) or by a planar cap at the lens equator if pseudophakic. That opening sits ~2 mm anterior of the Atchison posterior-lens vertex; the phakic capsule dents from there into the vitreous. Axial length sets spectacle refraction as SR = (AL − 23.58) / (−0.299) D, then the ellipsoid semi-axes Rx, Ry, Rz and vitreous depth. Occupying volume is the given mL (no Boyle / IOP). The bubble is axisymmetric about gravity and sits at the zenith. A Young–Laplace meridian is integrated along the real cavity wall (retina θ, and a second solve with lens/IOL θ where the interface meets the capsule or IOL). Gas–liquid σ and retina θ come from the cavity fluid: vitreous ~61 mN/m and 39°, aqueous ~65 mN/m and 30°, BSS ~72 mN/m and 30°. Lens θ is 50° phakic and 55° on an IOL (oil on lens 30°). Tamponade is contact if the break sits on the gas side of that meniscus. Force on gas-contact retina is ΔP = σ κ₀ + Δρ g (z − z_apex); it is zero on the fluid side.

Silicone oil uses the same cavity and YL solver with σ ≈ 32–40 mN/m (lower in vitreous than BSS), Δρ ≈ 30 kg/m³, and a 25° Young angle, so it stays rounded. 1000 cs and 5000 cs differ mainly in viscosity; the static shape is almost the same. The macula is a 2.75 mm-radius disk around the fovea. Clock hours are as you face the eye, like a clock: 12 up, 3 right (patient-left), 9 left (patient-right). The macula is at the back of the globe. The optic disc is 4.5 mm nasal to the fovea (toward 3 o’clock in OD, toward 9 o’clock in OS). Vascular arcades leave the disc and pass 4 mm above and below the fovea. The purple ring is the equator (17.5 mm nasal and 18.75 mm temporal from the anterior pole at 24 mm AL, +0.16 mm per mm AL). Clock hours are drawn on the equator. The pars plana ring is 4.3 mm nasal and 5.9 mm temporal from the lens/IOL cap at 24 mm AL, changing by 0.7 mm per millimetre of axial length. The vitreous base is a single line 2.5 mm posterior to that pars plana ring. The default tear sits on the vitreous-base line.

Bubble Sim does not store patient data and should not be used to make treatment or positioning decisions.
